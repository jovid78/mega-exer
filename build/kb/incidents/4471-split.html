How to tell them apart

  • Parser. Day and month swapped, from a tenant that writes the day first, through the app, email or a booking-v2 form.
  • Hint. Day and month swapped too, but only through the booking-v3 form, which tells people to type the month first. Same symptom as the parser, different fix.
  • One day. The date moves by a single day, or differs between two time zones. Not a swap. The desk already lists these in its notes and counts them under the parser, by tenant.

How they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line and nobody reads the body. These reports don’t share one subject line. Triage says to merge when unsure. The intake tag is guessed from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can be several bugs with one symptom. Read the bodies before you call it known.
  • Count by cause, not by subject.
